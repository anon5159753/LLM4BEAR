### LLM4BEAR Bundle Refinement Components

#### EGPO-derived Evaluation Module [[View Prompts](../1_EGPO)]

Comprises an ensemble of three refined prompts ($\mathcal{E}=\{e_1,e_2,e_3\}$). Each prompt, optimised through the EGPO mechanism, captures a distinct evaluation perspective:
- $e_1$. The "harsh" prompt is tasked with identifying faults and considering why a bundle might be low-quality.
- $e_2$. The "neutral" prompt acts as a neutral judge and tries to directly identify whether the bundle needs improvement.
- $e_3$. The "lenient" prompt focuses on identifying high-quality aspects of a bundle.

The Evaluation Module uses an ensemble strategy comprising three prompts; each induces the LLM to generate qualitative feedback $\mathbf{f} = \{f_1,f_2,f_3\}$ and a corresponding 5-point score. To ensure high-quality bundles, we adopt a conservative "veto" approach: a bundle is flagged as suboptimal unless all individual scores $s_i \in \mathbf{s}$ are 4 or above. For instance, in Figure~\ref{placeholder}, if $f_1$ identifies that the camera body in Bundle \#1 is effectively non-functional without a complementary lens, the LLM can pinpoint this missing synergy to justify a low score ($s_1 = 2$). This failure classifies the bundle as "Needs improvement", even if $s_2,s_3 \geq 4$. 

#### Summary Module

All feedback is synthesised in the Summary Module to determine a revised intent $\phi^\star$ (e.g., "Travel Photographer Essentials"), establishing a direction to guide subsequent refinement. This module decides which action to take **remove** inaccurate item or **add** item to complete an incomplete bundle.

#### Remove Module

The Remove Module eliminates items that conflict with the revised bundle intent $\phi^\star$ by leveraging qualitative feedback $\mathbf{f}$ from the Evaluation Module. It removes items that undermine bundle coherence to improve bundle quality. For our crowdsourced bundle example, the **Mophie Juice Pack Air Battery Case for iPhone 5 and 5S** would be **removed** as it does not aline with the revised "Travel Photographer Essentials" intent.

#### Add Module

To identify items relevant to the bundle, the Add Module leverages an LLM to generate $n=3$ candidate item descriptions based on the revised bundle intent $\phi^\star$. Descriptions are represented as tokenised feature lists~\cite{bao2025heterogeneoususermodelingllmbased} (e.g., [_Product Type: Camera Body_][_Brand: Canon_][_Cost-tier: Medium_][_Target User: Novice Photographer_]) and are converted into embeddings to retrieve the top-3 most semantically similar items from the dataset. Finally, a candidate pool is constructed from both the remaining items from the bundle's original session and the retrieved similar items from across the dataset. The most suitable item addition from the candidate pool is selected based on the revised bundle intent $\phi^\star$ and feedback $\mathbf{f}$ from the Evaluation Module. In our bundle example, where the revised intent may be "Travel Photographer Essentials", a 128GB SSD may need to be added. Other options may include a camera lens and camera maintenance set. While the framework may ultimately select the 128 SSD based on the revised bundle intent, this multi-candidate approach enables the consideration of higher-order item relations instead of merely capturing the most obvious first-order associations.
